REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Last updated: 14 September 2026. No payment is currently taken through this website. The “Request my Audit” action is an expression of interest only.

Paid work starts separately.

Before any paid engagement begins, scope, fee, payment timing, cancellation terms and approved third-party costs are confirmed in writing.

1. Business clients

Commercial Growth is intended primarily for business clients. The written engagement terms agreed for a paid project take priority. Unless those terms say otherwise, fees fairly attributable to work already performed are not ordinarily refundable merely because a client changes direction or a hoped-for commercial outcome is not achieved.

2. Before work begins

If a paid engagement is cancelled before work begins, amounts paid for work that will not be performed will normally be refundable, subject to any clearly agreed non-recoverable third-party costs.

3. If Commercial Growth cancels

If Commercial Growth cancels an engagement and cannot provide an agreed alternative, amounts paid for work that will not be supplied will be refunded.

4. Consumers

If Commercial Growth accepts a client who is legally acting as a consumer rather than for business purposes, mandatory consumer rights take priority. Distance service contracts can carry statutory cancellation rights, including a 14-day cancellation period in many circumstances. Any consumer engagement will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the information provided before the contract is concluded.

5. Service concerns

If you believe paid work was not delivered in accordance with the written scope, contact lauraqbusiness@gmail.com promptly so it can be reviewed against the agreed terms and applicable law.
