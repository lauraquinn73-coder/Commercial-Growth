BEFORE WE WORK TOGETHER

A few useful answers.

Who is Commercial Growth for?

Businesses that know there is more potential in their sales, customer journey, retention or commercial processes, but need a clearer view of where to focus first.

Do I need lots of data already?

No. We work with what exists. If the baseline is not clear enough yet, part of the work can be deciding what needs to be measured before making bigger changes.

Is this a marketing agency?

No. Marketing can be part of the customer journey, but Commercial Growth looks beyond acquisition. The focus is what happens from interest through sale, delivery, repeat business and retention.

What happens after the audit?

You can take the action plan and implement it internally, or continue with ongoing consultancy for hands-on support putting the priority changes in place.

How does pricing work?

Scope depends on the size of the business and what needs to be reviewed. After a short initial conversation, you will receive a clear scope and fee before any work begins.
