ACCESSIBILITY

Accessibility statement.

Last updated: 9 September 2026. Commercial Growth aims to make commercialgrowth.ie usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including people using keyboards, screen readers, zoom or reduced-motion settings.

Accessibility approach

The site has been designed with WCAG 2.2 AA principles in mind. This statement is not a certification of perfect conformance, and accessibility will be reviewed as the site changes.

  • semantic headings and landmarks are used throughout the site;
  • a “Skip to content” link is available for keyboard users;
  • interactive controls use visible focus indicators and descriptive labels;
  • the Commercial Growth Check can be completed with a keyboard;
  • the Check dialog manages focus and returns focus to the control that opened it;
  • text and primary controls use contrast intended to meet WCAG AA thresholds;
  • decorative logos use empty alternative text so they are not announced unnecessarily;
  • motion is reduced when the user has enabled a reduced-motion preference;
  • the site uses responsive layouts and supports text resizing without relying on a fixed desktop width.

Known limitations

Third-party services opened from the website, such as Gmail, Instagram, Formspree or Google services, are controlled by their respective providers and may have their own accessibility behaviour.

Need an alternative?

If any part of the website is difficult to use or you need information in another accessible format, email lauraqbusiness@gmail.com. Please describe the page or feature and the format or adjustment that would help.

Ongoing review

Accessibility should be retested whenever material design, content or form changes are introduced.
