COOKIE POLICY

Cookie Policy.

Last updated: 14 September 2026. This policy explains browser storage and optional analytics used by commercialgrowth.ie.

Optional analytics requires your choice.

Google Analytics is not loaded until you select “Allow optional analytics”. Rejecting analytics does not stop you using the site or Commercial Growth Check.

2. Analytics

Custom analytics events measure aggregate funnel stages such as starting the Check, completing it, viewing Audit information and requesting an Audit. The site is configured not to intentionally send contact details, free-text form answers or internal lead IDs in custom events. Page URLs sent to Analytics are stripped of query strings to reduce unnecessary data collection.

3. No advertising pixels

The current site does not use Google Ads conversion tags, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel or behavioural advertising cookies. Google Signals and advertising-personalisation signals are disabled in the site configuration.

4. Third-party embeds

The current site does not embed social feeds, maps, videos, chat widgets or advertising frames. Instagram is an external link. Formspree is contacted only when a submission is sent.

5. Change your choice

Use the Privacy choices control in the footer to reopen the consent banner. Withdrawing analytics consent disables further Analytics tracking from the site and the site attempts to remove first-party Google Analytics cookies it can access.

6. Browser controls

You can also delete cookies and site storage in your browser. Blocking necessary session storage may cause unfinished Check progress to be lost.
