ONGOING COMMERCIAL CONSULTANCY

Find the gap. Then make the change happen.

Ongoing consultancy is for businesses that want support implementing, testing and measuring the commercial priorities identified through the work.

What ongoing support can include

  • Sales-process and conversion improvements
  • Follow-up, reactivation and retention systems
  • Customer-journey improvements
  • Offer, package and revenue-stream development
  • Scripts, workshops and sales enablement
  • Commercial measurement and testing

Why it is scoped rather than packaged blindly

The right level of support depends on the business, the team, the available data and how much implementation is required. Scope, deliverables, access, fees and timing are therefore agreed in writing before paid work begins.

What it is not

Ongoing consultancy is not unlimited operational support, full social-media management, a guarantee of commercial results, or a substitute for legal, tax, accounting or regulated professional advice.

The Commercial Growth Check is the lowest-friction first step. It shows the areas I would want to investigate before discussing a larger engagement.
