PRIVACY POLICY

How your information is handled.

Last updated: 10 October 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled when you use commercialgrowth.ie, complete the on-device Commercial Growth Check, send an enquiry through Tally, use the optional public Website Scanner, or contact Commercial Growth.

Who is responsible for your data?

Commercial Growth is operated by Laura Quinn, sole trader, using CRO registered business name number 794953, who is the data controller for business enquiries. Business address: Ballyea, Ballina, County Tipperary, Ireland. Contact: lauraqbusiness@gmail.com.

1. What information is collected

The free Growth Check runs in your browser: its answers are used locally to generate a snapshot and are not automatically sent to Commercial Growth. If you choose to open and submit our separate Tally enquiry form, the information you enter in that form is received by Commercial Growth. Depending on what you submit or allow, this may include:

  • your name and business name entered into the Tally enquiry form;
  • your email address and any optional message you submit to Tally;
  • an optional public business website or social link you enter in the local Check or enquiry message;
  • high-level business information you choose to include in the enquiry message;
  • up to three broad, non-identifying focus-area codes, if you click the Audit enquiry link after completing the Check and the matching hidden fields have been enabled in Tally;
  • technical website usage information through Google Analytics only if you choose to allow analytics.

Please do not submit special-category or highly sensitive personal data, including health information, payment-card or bank details, passwords, government identifiers, or personal information about your customers or staff. The Check does not need that information.

3. Commercial Growth Snapshot and lead prioritisation

The site uses simple rule-based logic in your browser to organise your Check answers and surface areas that may be worth investigating. These detailed answers are not transmitted to Commercial Growth by completing the Check. If you choose the Audit enquiry link, up to three broad focus-area labels may be included as URL parameters; no name, email, free text or website URL is placed in that link. This is an initial screening tool only. It does not make a legal, financial, employment, credit or similarly significant decision about you, and it is not a substitute for a full commercial review.

4. Service providers and recipients

Personal data may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the website and respond to enquiries:

  • Tally BV (Belgium) provides the separate business enquiry form and processes your submitted form information on our behalf. Tally states that submissions are stored in Europe, and its published Data Processing Agreement applies to professional users under its account terms. Tally uses subprocessors and may process some data outside the EEA where optional features such as email notifications are enabled. The Tally form is opened only when you choose an enquiry link.
  • Google provides Gmail for email handling and Google Analytics 4 for optional website measurement. Gmail data-processing terms and processing locations depend on the type of Google account used. Analytics is not loaded until you consent.
  • GitHub Pages / GitHub hosts the public website and may process technical request information needed to provide and secure the hosting service.

These providers have their own terms and security arrangements. Some service providers or optional features may involve international transfers, for which a valid GDPR transfer mechanism must apply. We review provider terms and limit unnecessary integrations.

Public website scanner (when enabled)

If you choose to scan a public business homepage, its URL is sent to our scanning service hosted using Cloudflare Workers. The service requests the publicly available homepage and its robots.txt file and returns preliminary observations about visible HTML signals. The scanned website may record the automated visit in its own server logs. Cloudflare may process the URL and technical request information in service/security logs in accordance with its applicable terms and settings. The website scanner is not intended to collect passwords, private links, personal account pages, customer records or confidential data.

The scanner does not require an account, maintain a database of submitted URLs or scanned HTML, or use entered URLs for marketing enquiries. To prevent automated misuse, the scanner uses Cloudflare Turnstile verification and request rate limits when publicly enabled. Turnstile may process browser and network information to establish whether the request is legitimate. The scanning service may process IP addresses and requested website URLs transiently and through provider security and operational logs; exact log retention depends on the Cloudflare account configuration. Do not enter private URLs or confidential information. More information is available in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

5. Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics 4 is optional. If allowed, it may collect usage information such as session statistics, approximate geolocation, browser/device information and a first-party analytics identifier. Commercial Growth’s custom analytics events are deliberately limited to categorical funnel information and do not intentionally include your name, email address, business name, business link or free-text form answers.

See the Cookie Policy for the storage names, purposes, durations and how to change your choice.

6. Retention

  • Enquiries that do not become client engagements: ordinarily retained for up to 12 months from the last meaningful contact, then deleted unless there is a legitimate reason to keep them longer.
  • Client and transaction records: information needed for accounts, tax, contracts or legal claims may be retained for up to six years or longer where law or an active dispute requires it.
  • Check progress: stored in browser session storage and normally removed when the browser session ends or when you start a fresh Check.
  • Tally submissions: stored in the Tally account until manually reviewed and deleted under our retention schedule. For enquiries that do not lead to engagement, the usual review point is 12 months after the last meaningful contact unless lawful retention is needed.
  • Scanner security and diagnostic logs: security and operational records may be held by Cloudflare according to its configured retention periods; verify these settings before enabling public scanning.
  • Analytics consent choice: remembered for no more than six months before you are asked again.
  • Google Analytics: subject to the retention settings configured in the Analytics property and the cookie durations described in the Cookie Policy.

7. Your rights

Subject to the GDPR and applicable Irish data-protection law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, and in some cases data portability or withdrawal of consent. Withdrawing analytics consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal.

To exercise a right, email lauraqbusiness@gmail.com. Reasonable steps may be taken to verify identity before releasing or changing personal data.

8. Complaints

You can raise a concern directly with Commercial Growth first. You also have the right to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission. Information is available at dataprotection.ie.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to reduce the risk of loss, misuse or unauthorised access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not use the Commercial Growth Check to transmit confidential customer records, credentials, banking information or other sensitive material.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, processors or legal requirements change. The current version and date will be published on this page.
