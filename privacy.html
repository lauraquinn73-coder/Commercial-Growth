PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 14 September 2026. This notice explains how Laura Quinn, operating Commercial Growth, handles personal data submitted through commercialgrowth.ie.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller is Laura Quinn, operating Commercial Growth. Privacy enquiries can be sent to lauraqbusiness@gmail.com.

2. What data is collected?

The Commercial Growth Check asks for a name, business name, email address, a short description of the business, an optional public business URL and commercial questionnaire answers. If you submit the Check, limited source information such as UTM campaign parameters and the referring hostname may also be attached to the enquiry. The form does not ask for home addresses, payment details, health information or customer/staff records.

Please do not submit special-category personal data, passwords, confidential customer records, staff records or information you are not authorised to share.

3. Why the information is used and the lawful basis

  • Responding to an enquiry and taking steps before a possible contract: to review your Check, answer questions and discuss a possible Audit or consultancy engagement. The lawful basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where processing is necessary to take steps at your request before a contract.
  • Operating and protecting the service: preventing duplicate submissions, maintaining records of enquiries, troubleshooting and protecting the website. The lawful basis is legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f), balanced against your rights.
  • Optional analytics: Google Analytics is loaded only after you choose to allow optional analytics. The lawful basis for that optional processing is consent under Article 6(1)(a).
  • Legal and accounting obligations: if you become a paying client, some records may need to be retained to comply with tax, accounting or legal obligations under Article 6(1)(c).

4. The automated Commercial Growth Snapshot

The website uses questionnaire answers to generate a preliminary snapshot and an internal fit/priority indicator. These tools are used to organise an enquiry and suggest areas worth investigating. They do not make a solely automated decision that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and they do not automatically accept or reject you as a client. Any paid engagement is reviewed and agreed by Laura.

5. Who receives the data?

  • Formspree receives Check and Audit-request submissions as the form-processing provider.
  • Google Analytics receives limited website-use data only after analytics consent. Custom events are designed not to contain names, email addresses, business names, free-text answers or lead IDs.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the public website and may process technical request/log data as part of hosting.
  • Google/Gmail may process information if you choose to contact Commercial Growth by email.

6. International processing

Some service providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this occurs, the relevant provider is responsible for using an applicable transfer mechanism or safeguard. You can review the privacy information of Formspree, Google and GitHub for current details about their processing locations and safeguards.

7. How long information is kept

Unsuccessful or inactive enquiries are normally deleted or anonymised within 12 months of the last meaningful contact unless there is a legitimate reason to retain them longer. Browser draft answers are stored only in session storage while the Check is in progress and are removed after a successful submission. If you become a client, contractual, invoicing and accounting records may be retained for the period required by applicable law.

8. Is providing the information required?

You do not have to complete the Check. If you choose to enquire, enough information to identify the business and contact you is necessary to respond meaningfully. Optional fields are marked as optional.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You also have the right to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

10. Security

The site uses HTTPS, a restrictive Content Security Policy, data minimisation, input validation, limited browser storage and third-party form-spam controls. No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not send passwords or highly confidential material through the public Check.

11. Marketing

Completing the Commercial Growth Check does not subscribe you to a newsletter or marketing list. If Commercial Growth introduces marketing email in future, a separate lawful process and opt-out mechanism will be used.

12. Contact

For privacy requests, email lauraqbusiness@gmail.com.
