PRIVACY NOTICE

How your information is used.

Last updated: September 2026. This notice explains how Commercial Growth uses information submitted through commercialgrowth.ie.

Information you choose to provide

If you complete the Commercial Growth Check or contact Commercial Growth, you may provide your name, business name, email address, website or social link and information about the business, its customer journey and commercial processes.

Why it is used

The information is used to review your enquiry, understand whether Commercial Growth may be a suitable fit, respond to you, and provide the Commercial Growth service if you decide to proceed. Completing an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

Lead and acquisition information

The site may record non-sensitive attribution information such as the page you landed on, referral source and campaign parameters so Commercial Growth can understand which acquisition channels are producing enquiries. A random lead reference may be used to connect funnel stages without sending your name or email address to analytics tools.

Form processing

The Commercial Growth Check is designed for a secure third-party form processor such as Formspree. When enabled, that provider processes the information on behalf of Commercial Growth so the enquiry can be delivered and managed. Review the provider’s privacy information before enabling the live form.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 may be used to understand aggregate website performance, acquisition sources and funnel stages such as starting the Commercial Growth Check, completing it, viewing the Audit investment or requesting an Audit. Google Analytics is not loaded unless you choose to allow analytics in the privacy banner. Commercial Growth does not intentionally send your name, email address, business name, business link, free-text answers or internal lead reference to Google Analytics. You can choose “Necessary only” and still use the site and Commercial Growth Check.

Your analytics choice

Your analytics choice is stored in your browser so the site remembers it. You can reopen Privacy choices from the website footer and change your preference at any time.

Necessary storage

Session storage may be used to preserve progress in the Commercial Growth Check while you move through the questions. This is used for functionality rather than advertising.

How long information is kept

Enquiry information should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the enquiry, manage the commercial relationship, meet accounting or legal obligations, or establish and defend legal rights. Commercial Growth will periodically review and delete information that is no longer required.

Your rights

Depending on applicable data-protection law, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability. You can contact Commercial Growth about information you have submitted.
